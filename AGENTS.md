# NWW Reborn: working rules

- Lore: the First World War did not happen. Preserve the LTB compromise, dissolution, successor tags and existing focus/event IDs when extending content.
- Before designing or changing mechanics, inspect working examples in the installed HOI4 and its DLC. Prefer DLC patterns for national focuses, event choices, advisors, MIO traits, technology sharing and timed decisions. Record the concrete reference files in the change notes. This is an explicit user preference for future work.
- The legacy mod is now at `C:/Users/Eugene/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/nwwreborn/old-mod/noworldwarzhive` (project-relative `old-mod/noworldwarzhive`). Use this path for its LTB tree, localization, ideas and artwork. This is the user-designated source for future work. Adapt interesting concepts to the current lore and balance; do not blindly copy old syntax, huge factory rewards or obsolete technology effects.
- Use the installed game's documentation and definitions for the actual target version, including its resource/energy system. Validate effect, trigger, modifier and MIO-stat names.
- Treat tooltip evaluation as part of correctness. Keep election and ruling-party changes in a complete `set_politics` block, following the DLC example. Do not claim a runtime crash is verified fixed without a game reproduction.
- Advisor canvases are 65x67, but their visible artwork must fit inside the card with transparent bottom clearance. Check the occupied alpha bounds, not just the texture dimensions. Keep large leader portraits separate.
- Keep continuous-focus panels well below the last focus row and check coordinates after adding content.
- Avoid prerequisite connectors crossing unrelated focus branches. For distant cross-branch gates such as LTB's compromise requirement for the general staff, use explicit `has_completed_focus` conditions in `available`, retain the gameplay restrictions and validate them.
- LTB army reorganisation must require the Vilnius compromise and a resolved crisis with the Union preserved. Political transitions require at least 40% popularity for the target party, a viable way to build that popularity and preparatory focuses; retain substantive follow-up institutions and policy choices.
- The Vilnius compromise itself resolves LTB's crisis and stops its deadline. Bypass the redundant crisis focus through explicit `bypass` conditions, without its completion reward or dissolution event; retain a safe old-save settlement repair. Do not use `bypass_if_unavailable` with date-gated crisis availability: that could skip an unresolved crisis before its start date.
- Gate peaceful LTB progression on the completed compromise and absence of a triggered dissolution, rather than auxiliary settlement flags alone. Older saves can contain the completed compromise with missing flags; repair those through `on_daily_LTB` and keep crisis safeguards independent of the flags.
- Give local enterprises distinct roles and focus-gated progression. AAT MIOs and non-AAT designers/industrial concerns should have useful, coherent alternatives; preserve generic template traits when extending MIOs.
- Focus branches should develop one persistent national spirit by modifying its parameters. Keep temporary spirits separate. For MKN, use one shared spirit for the economy and one each for army, air force and navy, including their mutually exclusive programs.
- Preserve monarch Makhno and his portrait. The communist-only restriction applies to his field marshal and military advisor roles, not to his existing monarch role.
- User preferences authorize project-local technical resizing/cropping and DDS conversion of UI assets. Use imagegen for new or creatively redrawn raster artwork; preserve reference identity and save project-bound originals and prompts in the workspace.
- Run `python tools/validate_country_content.py --game "D:/Steam/steamapps/common/Hearts of Iron IV"` and `git diff --check` after content changes. Static validation does not replace checking a new campaign and a fresh game log.
- Keep country content in separate country-owned files such as `TUR_focuses.txt`, `TUR_ideas.txt`, `TUR_events.txt`, and `TUR_effects.txt`. Native filename overrides may contain a comment pointing to the country tree. Shared bilateral definitions should have a documented owner.
- Historical LTB AI must take `LTB_consolidate_the_union` and `LTB_the_vilnius_compromise` as its first two focuses and preserve the Union. Historical Austrian AI prioritizes the Hungarian ultimatum and conquest; retain a real equipped starting army and political/war gates.
- Legacy percentage bonuses should be meaningful, normally in steps of 5–10 percentage points. Daily political drift, flat political power and other absolute modifiers are not percentage bonuses.
- For archive content, also run `python tools/validate_legacy_content.py --game "D:/Steam/steamapps/common/Hearts of Iron IV"`. Compatibility registries must collect direct focus/shared-focus definitions only, never recursively collect every `id`: event IDs and province IDs must not become focus tokens.
- Use a leading zero in all script decimals (`0.5`, `-0.05`). HOI4 1.19.3 rejects bare `.5` values in effects even when the static Clausewitz parser accepts them.
- Imperial Germany starts with the 19 overseas states listed in `docs/GER_overseas_setup.json`, including the modern subdivisions of Namibia and German New Guinea. Keep indigenous cores; do not grant German colonial cores or transfer British Papua, Dutch New Guinea, American Samoa or Guam.
- Germany's NWW economy must not activate MEFO, conquest-economy debt, renewal missions or repayment penalties. Keep guarded old-save cleanup and run `python tools/validate_runtime_setup.py` for these changes and LTB generated focus icons.
- Ruanda and Burundi must retain German ownership with Gotterdammerung enabled. Check `transfer_state_to` and other DLC setup effects as well as `owner`: the original WUW initializers silently handed these states to Congo after reading ownership.
- Keep LTB's entire general-staff branch aligned at row zero, with its compromise/crisis requirements in availability conditions and no long cross-branch connector.
- Austrian Hungarian/Croatian campaigns are full-conquest objectives. Keep annexation restricted to actual capitulation to Austria after the corresponding war focus, whole-country claims/peace AI, unfinished-campaign repair, and the ownership-gated Austria-Hungary formation focus. Do not annex a neighbor in peacetime or because a different country defeated it.
- Historical Ottoman AI takes the surviving dynasty's Balkan route: internal council accords and army preparation, full Bulgarian conquest and settlement, then Greece, then wider Balkan pressure. Preserve all archived TUR focus IDs; do not restore WWI/Kemalist lore or the monarchy-only path's old fascist prerequisite.
- Ottoman council accords require paid, serialized negotiations. Keep one crisis spirit with successive stages, refundable cancellation and 40% mandates on political changes. Explicit TUR AI rules override history; support coherent random and nonhistorical plans, including without Battle for the Bosporus.
- Kuwait starts owned by TUR with its KUW core retained. British subject initializers must not recreate a KUW dependency. Balkan integration requires actual ownership/control, and campaign annexation requires actual capitulation to TUR after the corresponding focus. Run `python tools/validate_turkey_balkans.py` for this content.
- LTB has 48 of 96 focuses at 70 days. Preserve the 35-day opening and existing compromise timing; the combined minimum route through the principal starting debuffs is 945 days, reaching August 1938. Keep `docs/LTB_pacing.json` consistent with focus costs and prerequisites.
- Soviet lore follows the README's Great Tumult of 1918–1922: internal land, food, industrial and constitutional crises caused revolution without World War I. Keep `SOV_focus` and the 14 original NWW focus IDs, with country content in `SOV_focuses.txt` and related country-owned files.
- SOV uses its own successive state/economy/army spirits, not vanilla paranoia or purge subsystems. Preserve compatibility IDs but isolate native initialization, decisions, GUI, events, spirits and AI. Save repair must restore actual reform stages without repeating rewards or annexing land. Run `python tools/validate_soviet_content.py --game "D:/Steam/steamapps/common/Hearts of Iron IV"`.
- Soviet western pressure opens in 1939 and its prepared historical route reaches a war goal in 1940. Diplomacy requires recipient consent or refusal. New campaigns must preserve allied status and accepted `MKN_equal_autonomy`; Makhno's successors are addressed without assuming he remains alive after his funeral event.
